title: This and that
date: 2026-06-21
tags: ["General Information"]
---

This side = the side you’re on<br>
That side = the side you’re not on

If partway between:

This side = the side you’re closest to<br>
That side = the side you’re farthest from

<h3>Clarification</h3>

Further or furthest = the idea of distance<br>
“Nothing could be further from the truth.”

Farther or farthest = actual physical distance<br>
“Town A is farther away than Town B.”
